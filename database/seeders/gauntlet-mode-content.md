Gauntlet Mode is PokéVoid's classic run structure — 90 waves (or 110 for the final secret boss) of increasingly difficult battles, all compressed into a single run. Unlike Chaos Mode's branching Battle Path, Gauntlet runs follow a fixed-but-seeded sequence of encounters that always ends at wave 90 with the Champion. It is the mode every new player starts with, and it contains the unlock chain that opens every other mode in the game.

---

## Gauntlet variants

All Gauntlet modes share the same 90-wave structure and the same fixed encounter positions. What differs between them is the rule set applied on top.

| Mode | In-game name | How to unlock | Key rule |
|------|-------------|---------------|----------|
| **Gauntlet Journey** | Classic | Available from the start after catching 5 Pokémon (Starter Catch Quest) | Standard rules. Use any Pokémon you have caught or hatched. Fusions cost ΩGOLD. |
| **Gauntlet Rogue** | Draft | Unlocked by completing the Nuzlight Unlock Quest | Draft start — you receive 2 random Pokémon to begin with. ΩGOLD can reroll options. |
| **Gauntlet Nuzlight** | Nuzlight | Unlocked via quest by defeating your 4th-stage Rival (wave 55) in any mode | No healing items purchasable in shops. Fainted Pokémon revive at wave 10 milestones. |
| **Gauntlet Nuzlocke** | Nuzlocke | Unlocked via quest after winning 2 Nuzlight runs | Full Nuzlocke rules — fainted Pokémon are permanently removed from the party. |
| **Gauntlet Nuzlight Rogue** | Nuzlight Draft | Unlocked after 2 Nuzlight wins (any variant) | Nuzlight rules + Rogue draft start. |
| **Gauntlet Nuzlocke Rogue** | Nuzlocke Draft | Unlocked after 2 Nuzlocke wins (any variant) | Nuzlocke rules + Rogue draft start. |
| **The Void** | Nightmare | Unlocked by defeating all 28 rivals across any runs | 500-wave epic with progressively escalating rule changes and up to 5 simultaneous rivals. See below. |

## How a Gauntlet run works

### Starting out

At the start of a run in Gauntlet Journey, you pick your team from any Pokémon you have previously caught or hatched. All starters begin at level 5. Your starting money is ₽1,000 by default (up to ₽3,000 with Omega Items that grant money bonuses, which are consumed on use).

In **Gauntlet Rogue**, you instead receive two Pokémon drafted at random. You can spend ΩGOLD to reroll your options before locking in.

### The wave structure

Every 10 waves is a boss wave. Every 20 waves within each 30-wave block (at wave 20, 50, 80, etc.) is a Gym Leader / trainer boss wave — a fixed trainer encounter rather than a random wild or trainer fight. In between are random wild encounters and occasional random trainer battles (roughly every 4 waves when conditions are met).

**Random trainer frequency:** A trainer battle can appear on any wave where: the wave index is divisible by 4, the wave is not a boss wave or fixed battle, and no other fixed battle occurs within 3 waves of that position.

### Post-battle rewards

After every wild or trainer battle you receive a choice of 3 items (the shop). After every 10th-wave boss and after rival battles you receive two item choices. A Move Upgrade choice also appears after rival battles and other dynamic events if Move Upgrades are enabled for the run.

Every 100th wave (wave 1 of each new century in Nightmare) you automatically receive an EXP Share.

Occasionally after non-fixed battles there is a small random chance of a bonus Glitch Piece, Relic Gold, or Big Nugget drop.

## Fixed encounter structure

The run generates its fixed encounters at the start using a seeded random system. Wave numbers for rivals, Elite Four, major bosses, and evil team encounters are all randomised within defined ranges each run — so you never know exactly which wave they will appear on, only the approximate zone.

### Rivals (primary)

Your primary rival for the run is assigned randomly from the pool of rivals you have defeated. Each stage appears within a fixed wave range:

| Rival stage | Wave range |
|------------|------------|
| Stage 1 | Waves 1–15 |
| Stage 2 | Waves 16–30 |
| Stage 3 | Waves 31–45 |
| Stage 4 | Waves 46–60 |
| Stage 5 | Waves 61–75 |
| Stage 6 (final) | Wave 90 exactly |

Each stage is placed randomly within its range. Stage 6 is always locked to wave 90.

### Secondary rivals (corrupted)

If you have defeated at least one rival, a secondary rival (drawn from your pool of defeated rivals) also appears in the run. Secondary rivals appear at stages 2–6, using slightly tighter wave ranges (capped at wave 89 for their stage 6 rather than 90). Secondary rivals are shown in a corrupted visual state by default unless you have unlocked **The Void Overtaken** achievement, at which point they appear normally.

### Elite Four and Champion

Five fixed trainer battles representing the Elite Four and Champion are placed randomly within waves 30–89 (avoiding waves already claimed by rivals or major bosses):

| Position | Trainer tier |
|----------|-------------|
| 1st placed | Elite Four member 1 |
| 2nd placed | Elite Four member 2 |
| 3rd placed | Elite Four member 3 |
| 4th placed | Elite Four member 4 |
| 5th placed (highest wave) | Champion |

The specific trainer types for each slot are generated fresh each run from the full Elite Four and Champion pools.

### Major bosses

Between waves 40 and 90, one or two Major Boss encounters are placed at random. The second major boss must be at least 20 waves away from the first. Major bosses are powerful multi-form encounters that heal and change form at low HP before being fully defeated. Defeating them provides valuable item rewards.

### Evil team encounters

Two clusters of evil team battles are seeded into the run:

**First cluster (early run):**
- 3 Evil Team Grunts: waves 1–25
- 1 Evil Team Admin: after the last grunt, up to wave 35
- 1 Evil Team Boss (first form): after the admin, up to wave 40

**Second cluster (mid-to-late run):**
- 2 Evil Team Grunts: randomly placed in waves 40–89 (from a 5-wave window)
- 2 Evil Team Admins: placed after the grunts in the same window
- 1 Evil Team Boss (second form): placed last in the same window

The specific evil team (Rocket, Galactic, Plasma, etc.) is randomised each run.

### The Smitty final battle

There is a **25% chance** (configurable by server overrides) that wave 90 spawns a Smitty Battle instead of your rival's stage-6 encounter. If this happens, you fight Smitty as the run's final boss rather than your rival. Winning still ends the run normally at wave 90, but triggers a different victory sequence — the Smitty victory cutscene, potential Universal Smitty Form unlocks, and the standard run-clear rewards. The run does not continue past wave 90 in either case.

## Unlock chain

Gauntlet modes unlock in sequence. Each unlock is triggered by completing a specific task:

**Gauntlet Journey** — available after completing the Starter Catch Quest (catch 5 Pokémon). This quest is acquired from the Omega Shop after catching your very first Pokémon.

**Gauntlet Nuzlight** — a quest unlocks automatically when you defeat your 4th-stage Rival (wave 55+) for the first time in any mode. Win 1 run with this quest active to unlock Nuzlight Mode.

**Gauntlet Nuzlocke** — once you are in Nuzlight Mode, a quest unlocks when you defeat the 4th-stage Rival again. Win 2 Nuzlight runs (any variant, including Chaos Nuzlight) to complete it and unlock Nuzlocke Mode.

**Gauntlet Rogue (Draft)** — unlocked when Nightmare Mode is unlocked, or separately by completing specific draft-related quests.

**Gauntlet Nuzlight Rogue** — unlocked after winning 2 Nuzlight runs total across any variant (Nuzlight, Chaos Nuzlight, Chaos Nuzlight Midnight, Chaos Nuzlight FTL all count).

**Gauntlet Nuzlocke Rogue** — unlocked after winning 2 Nuzlocke runs total across any variant.

**The Void (Nightmare Mode)** — unlocked by defeating all 28 rivals at least once across your runs. When the last rival is defeated, a cutscene plays and Nightmare Mode is granted.

## The Void (Nightmare Mode)

The Void is a 500-wave ultra-hard Gauntlet run that builds on everything in the base modes. It uses the same 90-wave structure — but repeated five times in escalating segments of 100 waves each, with a new rival introduced and new rule changes applied at each segment boundary.

### Structure overview

| Segment | Waves | Shop available? | Nuzlocke active? | Rivals |
|---------|-------|----------------|-----------------|--------|
| 1 | 1–100 | Yes | No | 1 |
| 2 | 101–200 | No (shop removed) | No | 2 |
| 3 | 201–300 | No | No | 3 |
| 4 | 301–400 | No | Yes (Nuzlocke) | 4 |
| 5 | 401–500 | No | Yes (Nuzlocke) | 5 |

The shop is removed from wave 100 onward for the rest of the run. Full Nuzlocke rules (permanent death) activate from wave 300 onward.

### Rival escalation

At the start the run uses 1 rival. Each new 100-wave segment adds one more rival from the full pool of 28, up to a maximum of 5 simultaneously active rivals. Each rival appears at stages 2–6 within their assigned 100-wave segment, and each stage appears once. The newest rival (added most recently to the pool) gets priority placement at stage 6 in the segment where they are introduced.

Remaining rivals from the pool that aren't selected as the 5 primaries are still distributed across the run as lower-priority encounters.

### Major bosses in The Void

One Major Boss encounter is placed in each 100-wave segment, starting from wave 50 in the first segment and from the segment midpoint in subsequent ones.

### Evil team encounters in The Void

Each 100-wave segment contains its own evil team cluster: 2 grunts, 2 admins, and 1 boss, placed within that segment's wave range.

### Elite Four in The Void

Each segment also contains its own Elite Four cluster of 5 members (4 Elite Four + 1 Champion), placed randomly within that segment's available waves.

### Final wave (500)

Wave 500 always contains a Smitty Battle — replacing the expected rival final encounter in the last segment. This is the only fixed element in The Void that doesn't vary by seed.

### Dynamic Mode modifiers in The Void

Some individual battles in The Void can carry Dynamic Mode modifiers (the same system used in Chaos Mode). The number of simultaneous modifiers increases with wave depth. These modifiers are not permanent for the whole run — they apply only to the specific battle where they appear. Modifiers that can appear include Nuzlocke, Chaos Trainer, No Stat Boosts, Inverted Types, and others from the full modifier pool.

## Gauntlet-specific mechanics

### Gym Leader waves

Within each 30-wave block, wave 20 (or wave 30 if the run uses an offset-gym setting) is always a trainer boss — essentially a gym leader equivalent. These are fixed trainer fights rather than random encounters, and defeating them counts toward the trainer-boss-win stat.

### Boss waves (every 10)

Every 10th wave is a boss encounter. Boss Pokémon have a shield mechanic — they survive one killing blow and temporarily become immune to damage, then fully heal before the fight resumes. Trainer boss battles at wave 10 milestones follow the same multi-form logic.

### Rival stages and team scaling

Rivals scale their team with each stage encounter. A Stage 1 rival carries a small team of low-level Pokémon. By Stage 6, their team is fully built out with their signature Pokémon and several strong partners. Between stages, the rival's party grows in both size and power level.

### The Void Overtaken

Winning the game for the first time (defeating the Champion at wave 90 in any mode) grants the **The Void Overtaken** achievement and several permanent unlocks:
- Smitty Nuggets (a special currency)
- Nugget of Smitty
- Many More Nuggets
- Universal Smitty Forms become available if not yet unlocked

The Void Overtaken flag also enables corrupted secondary rivals to display without the corruption filter — showing them as normal rivals instead.

## Earning Glitch Quests

The core Gauntlet modes are the primary source of **Glitch Quests** — the quests that permanently unlock powerful Glitch Forms for specific Pokémon. Defeating a rival for the first time causes a Glitch Quest for that rival's associated Pokémon to appear in the Omega Shop (or the regular shop). Purchasing and completing it unlocks that Glitch Form permanently across all runs and modes.

Since all 28 rivals must be defeated to unlock The Void, and each defeat can trigger a new quest, working through the Gauntlet is also the main path to building out your Glitch Form collection.
